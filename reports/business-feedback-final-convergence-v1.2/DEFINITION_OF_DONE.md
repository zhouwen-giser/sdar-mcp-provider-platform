CI and deterministic tests do not replace live workflows. No startup DDL, silent fallback, synthetic route, hidden simulation truth or weapon action.

End-to-end ready additionally requires external SDAR consuming the same candidate Context/SSE/Input/Intervention. Missing external SDAR is EXTERNAL_PENDING and cannot be called end-to-end ready. Individual external blockers do not stop independent implementation.
